    About Henry Brewer, 14-year-old developer

    Henry Brewer is a 14-year-old developer from Austin, Texas. He goes to Alpha High School and builds AI projects in his spare time, like Vurn AI, a tool that scans code for security holes, and Lily, a voice AI that calls his grandma every morning. He helped start Crawft and was its CTO before selling his stake. He also built SiteLight, a side project he got to its first of revenue in 10 days. He has won a few hackathons and has merged pull requests in SymPy, Biome and Astro.
